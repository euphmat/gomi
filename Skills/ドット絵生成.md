# JRPG Ultra-Detailed High-Density Pixel Art Icon Generator
Please generate a single, high-resolution image featuring a structured **5x5 grid** of individual, highly detailed pixel art icons.

## Image Requirements
- **Format:** Single image containing the entire 5x5 grid.
- **Icon Type:** Detailed item icons, not just small sprites.
- **Text:** **None**
- **Image Size:** Each icon in the grid must be square (e.g., designed with a base grid of 128x128 pixels per icon).
- **Icon Background Color:** Light gray background for each icon cell.
- **Table:** Each icon must be contained within a clearly defined cell with a border.
- **Table Border:** 1px black border.
- **Style:**
    - **Refined late 16-bit to early 32-bit console RPG pixel art style** (e.g., high-quality SNES/PS1 hybrid).
    - **Ultra-detailed, high-resolution pixel art for icons** (very high pixel density, focusing on intricate detail).
    - **Sophisticated material textures:** complex crystalline structures, elaborate metal work with filigree, realistic scale patterns, detailed fabric folds, and nuanced gem surfaces.
    - **Sophisticated pixel-shading:** subtle use of anti-aliasing and pixel-shading techniques for depth and realism. Clear highlights and shadows define every form. Avoid flat colors.
    - **Clear outlines and meticulous attention to detail.**
